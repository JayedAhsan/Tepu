# Tepu
**Tepu is a lightweight desktop companion that sits on your screen, plays around, takes naps, reminds you about your screen time, and keeps you company while you work or browse.**

### The Story Behind Tepu
Tepu was my pet cat who sadly passed away. I created this project to remember him by, recreating his playful habits and personality as a companion for desktop.
